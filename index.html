Infinity Zoom Support

Need help with Infinity Zoom? Before troubleshooting, make a backup of artwork you want to keep: open Settings → Data → Export a local drawing backup. Gallery backups up to 64 MB can be imported. For a larger gallery, open each canvas and choose Canvas settings → Back up this canvas.

Contact support

Jacob@apgnashville.com

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and what happened. Do not send private artwork unless it is necessary to explain the issue.

Backups and restoring

Infinity Zoom stores your work on your device. You can export an editable backup to Files, then import a compatible backup from the gallery. Imported drawings are added as new copies. Preferences and palettes are not restored with a drawing backup.

Purchases

Manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account settings. In the app, choose Restore Purchases if a completed purchase is not appearing.
