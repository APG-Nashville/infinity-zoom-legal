Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Infinity Zoom is a drawing app for iPhone and iPad. You do not need to create an account to use the app.

Your drawings and app data

Your drawings, saved views, colors, palettes, and preferences are stored on your device. Infinity Zoom does not operate a service that receives your artwork, and the app does not include advertising or analytics services.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment information. Infinity Zoom keeps verified purchase status on your device to decide which features are available. Individual sticker-pack checkout is not offered in the first release.

Exports and backups

When you export artwork or a backup, Infinity Zoom creates a file and opens Apple’s share sheet. The service or person you choose in that sheet handles the shared copy under its own policies. Files you place in iCloud, device backups, or another storage service are governed by your device settings and that provider.

Deleting local data

You can move canvases to Trash and permanently delete them, or remove local artwork, colors, palettes, and preferences from Settings. Deleting local data does not cancel an Apple subscription. You must separately manage files you have exported, sent to others, or included in a device backup.

Support

If you contact support, APG Nashville LLC receives the information you choose to send. We use support correspondence to respond to your request and retain it only as long as reasonably needed for support, record-keeping, or legal obligations.
